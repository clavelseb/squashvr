  • Volley feed
  • Loose ball
  • Boast feed
  • Boast practice
  • Drive practice

Targets are worked out for each feed from where you will take the ball. Volley, loose-ball and boast-feed drills show a nick target on the front wall. Boast practice feeds deep into the back corner and shows a target on the side wall. Drive practice feeds down your wall to the back of the service box: the deep-drive target shows where to aim at your usual pace, and each drive is scored on length (a touch long beats short), how close it stays to the side wall all the way back, and touching the side wall late or not at all. Pace is shown but not scored. Each drill can be set to crosscourt nick, straight nick, boast, deep drive or no target.

Ball & racquet physics

Ball
24 g, 40 mm hollow shell, drag + Magnus lift
Bounce
COR 0.49 at 10 m/s, 45 °C, falling with speed. Always warm by default (40, 45 or 50 °C), or start cold and warm it up
Contact
Soft contact, ~5 ms on the strings, grip and spin on every wall and the floor: a gripping ball leaves rolling, keeping about 65 % of its speed along the surface
Hit assist
The swing and the ball's path are traced through each frame; a ball that would clip the frame or just miss the head is put on the strings (off, 3, 6 or 10 cm in the menu)
Nick
Emerges from the ball flattening into the wall–floor joint; targets are solved and verified with the same physics
Racquet
Built from your CAD model: open throat with bridge, 500 cm² head, 16×20 strings, 8×18 mm frame; 135 g, balance 353 mm, string-bed trampoline, frame mishits
Solver
Adaptive substeps, from 1 ms in flight down to 2 µs in hard impacts, swing interpolated between frames: the same shot gives the same result at 72, 90 or 120 Hz

Runs at 120 Hz where your headset allows it (Quest 2 offers 72, 90 or 120 Hz; there is no 100 Hz mode). Clear about 2 × 2 m of floor and keep your guardian on: you will swing hard.
